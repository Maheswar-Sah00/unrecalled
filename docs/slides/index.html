Unrecalled
People and agent together

The agent does the work. You make the calls.

It acts, not just advises

Scans the car, reads thousands of complaints, drafts a federal defect petition (49 CFR Part 552) and takes you to it.

It never invents a number

Grouping, coverage and the 10-point petition checklist are plain code. The model explains and drafts.

Your corrections stick

Exclude a complaint that doesn’t fit and every future scan remembers it.

Filing waits for a human yes

Marking a petition filed always stops for approval, whoever asks. The agent can’t skip it.
